# Card types

Every card has a type. The default is **Note**, and a map made entirely of
notes behaves exactly like a normal canvas. The other five types exist so
the app can tell the parts of your reasoning apart.

**Decision** — a question you need to commit to. "Which hosting provider?"
Decisions are the anchors a map is usually built around.

**Option** — a candidate answer to a decision. Options are where the
trade-offs live, so they tend to collect assumptions and risks beneath them.

**Assumption** — something you believe but have not verified. This is the
most useful type in the set. Assumptions are where decisions quietly go
wrong: not because the reasoning was bad, but because something taken for
granted turned out not to be. Naming them makes them checkable later.

**Risk** — something that could go wrong. Writing a risk down does not
make it less likely, but it does stop it being a surprise, and it keeps
the trade-off honest.

**Evidence** — something you actually know, with a source. Evidence is what
turns an assumption into a fact. An option with no evidence under it is a
preference, not a conclusion.

You do not need to type every card, and there is no correct ratio. Type the
ones that carry weight and leave the rest as notes.
