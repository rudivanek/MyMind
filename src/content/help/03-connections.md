# Connections

In most mapping tools a line means "these two things are related" and
nothing more. You are left to remember the rest.

In MyMind a connection carries a comment — a short line explaining *why*
one card leads to another. "Only if we stay under 1k users." "Contradicts
the pricing research." "This is the assumption everything else rests on."

That comment is the argument. The cards are just the pieces it connects.

It matters most when you come back. A map you made three months ago is
still readable if the connections explain themselves; without them you get
a diagram you have to reconstruct from memory, which is usually worse than
having written nothing.

There is a discipline to this that is worth adopting early: if you cannot
write a short reason on a connection, you may not have a reason yet. The
blank comment is itself a finding.
